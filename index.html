About Little Joy

A second home where curiosity leads the way

Little Joy began with a simple question from our founder, Anjali Sharma, a Montessori-trained teacher: what if school felt like a second home? After years of teaching, she saw that children bloom when they are trusted to explore, make choices and learn at their own pace.

In 2012 she opened a small classroom with eight children, a shelf of wooden materials and a big promise to protect every child's curiosity. Today that classroom has grown into a warm community of families, but the promise is exactly the same.

Our classrooms are calm, beautiful and child-sized. Children choose their work, move freely and learn through hands-on materials, guided by teachers who watch, listen and nurture.
